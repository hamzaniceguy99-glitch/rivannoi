The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, built on video of your own paddling and your own water.

Program 01 · Power from the core

Stroke & efficiency

Nearly everyone starts by pulling with their arms. We rebuild the stroke around torso rotation and a clean catch, fix posture and foot bracing, and work on paddle angle — which together turn paddling from a workout into something you can sustain for hours.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginners up
  • 2 sessions a week
  • Your own boat or board

Start this program

Written down, before you're standing at the launch.

Program 02 · Deciding before you launch

Wind, water & weather

Most paddling trouble is a decision made on shore. We work on reading marine and lake forecasts, understanding what wind does to open water, how wind against tide builds chop, and how to set a personal limit and respect it.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • Local forecasts

Start this program

Program 03 · Longer days, no drama

Trip planning

A good day on the water is mostly planning. We build routes with escape options, time them around wind and tide, sort out what to carry and how to keep it dry, and put together a float plan someone ashore actually has.

  • 8 weeks
  • Confident basics
  • 3 hours a week
  • Charts or maps

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Routes

Distance, exposure and bail-out points.

Weeks 3–4 · Timing

Working with wind and tide instead of against them.

Weeks 5–6 · Kit

Packing, dry bags and what lives on your person.

Weeks 7–8 · A real trip

Planned, run, then reviewed.

Weeks 3–4 · Shoulders

Stability work that prevents the common injuries.

Weeks 5–6 · Core

Weeks 7–8 · Endurance

Building toward longer days.

Program 04 · Shoulders that last

Paddling fitness

Paddling asks for thoracic rotation and shoulder stability, and most of us sit at desks all week. We build a short land program for both, plus endurance work, so the limiting factor becomes daylight rather than your back.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 sessions a week
  • Floor space
